01 / Profile

Cross-domain execution anchored in structure

Rather than specializing in a single narrow vertical, I operate at the intersection of strategy, software automation, and physical operations.

Running multiple companies simultaneously requires more than effort—it requires deliberate architecture. I treat every business challenge as a system: mapping bottlenecks, designing repeatable workflows, and automating routine execution so human attention stays focused on high-leverage decisions.

To maintain context and precision across disparate industries—from Norwegian health supplement compliance to hardware diagnostics and vending machine telemetry—I built Central Intelligence, a structured second-brain and AI operating layer that unifies company knowledge, metrics, and decision rules.

My standard is simple: clarity over complexity, verifiable data over assumptions, and durable systems over temporary fixes.
